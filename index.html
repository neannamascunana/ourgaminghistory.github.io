STARDEW VALLEY

Stardew Valley is an open-ended, country-life RPG and simulation game where you inherit your grandfather's old farm, clearing land to grow crops, raise animals, and build a thriving homestead. Players escape corporate life to live off the land, exploring mines, fishing, crafting, and building relationships with Pelican Town residents. It is created by American game developer Eric Barone, widely known by his online alias ConcernedApe. He acted as the sole developer, designer, musician, and artist for the game, working on it for over four years before releasing it in 2016.

What do you do in Stardew Valley?

It includes core activities such as planting crops, raising animals, fishing, mining for ore, fighting monsters, and socializing with Pelican Town villagers. It is an open-ended, relaxing life-simulation game where you aim to restore the local Community Center, earn money, and expand your farmstead

FARMING

the core mechanic, involving clearing land, planting seasonal seeds, watering daily, and harvesting crops for profit. Players use hoes, watering cans, and fertilizers, improving efficiency by leveling up skills, unlocking sprinklers, and managing livestock like cows and chickens to create a thriving agricultural operation.

CHARACTERS (The Villagers)

There are over 30 residents in Pelican Town, each with unique daily routines, personalities, and backstories that you can discover by talking to them and giving gifts.

  • Marriage Candidates (12 total): You can befriend, date, and marry these characters.
  • Bachelorettes: Abigail (adventurous gamer), Emily (eccentric/creative), Haley (fashionable photographer), Leah (artistic), Maru (nerdy inventor), Penny (kind teacher)
  • Bachelors: Alex (athletic jock), Elliott (poetic writer), Harvey (local doctor), Sam (music-loving skater), Sebastian (reclusive programmer), Shane (struggling, relatable).
  • Villagers/NPCs: There are over 28 inhabitants with unique routines. Key NPCs include Linus (friend to the wild), Mayor Lewis, Robin (carpenter), Pierre (general store owner), and Pam (bus driver).

MULTIPLAYER

multiplayer allows 1–8 players (PC) or 1–4 players (consoles) to operate a shared farm, offering cooperative farming, fishing, mining, and socializing. Players can join via online invite codes or split-screen, sharing a common world where they can earn money together or separately.

KEY MULTIPLAYER FEATURES

  • Shared World: Players share money, resources, and community center progress.
  • Finances: You can choose to have shared or separate wallets (changed at the town ledger in Mayor Lewis's house).
  • Split-Screen: Supported on Switch, PC, and consoles, but not allowed simultaneously with online players.
  • Player Responsibilities: Farmhands can only play when the host is online, whereas the host can play alone.

REC ROOM

Rec Room is a free, cross-platform social sandbox game where players can hang out, play millions of user-created games, and build their own virtual worlds. It functions like a virtual "rec center," featuring active, cooperative, and creative experiences accessible on VR headsets, mobile devices, and consoles.

In Rec Room, you can play, create, and socialize in a virtual, cross-platform world. Key activities include playing community-made games, competing in original quests like Laser Tag and Paintball, designing custom rooms with the Maker Pen, and customizing your avatar. It is a social platform where you can hang out with friends, explore millions of user-created rooms, and attend events.

  • CREATE AND BUILD - Use the Maker Pen tool to build custom, interactive rooms, objects, and inventions.
  • SOCIALIZE - Meet new people or hang out with friends in the Rec Center, communicate via voice/text, and visit user-created lounges.
  • CUSTOMIZE - Customize your avatar's appearance with clothes, hair, and accessories to express your style.
  • EXPLORE - Discover millions of rooms built by other creators, ranging from social hangouts to intense games.

MINECRAFT

Minecraft is a 3D sandbox video game where players explore, craft, build, and survive in an infinite world made entirely of blocks. Often compared to digital LEGOs, there are no set rules or linear objectives, allowing players to set their own goals, from farming and building giant castles to fighting monsters.

KEY ACTIVITIES:

  • BUILDING - Constructing houses, cities, or massive structures.
  • EXPLORING & MINING - Digging deep for materials like diamonds and exploring caves, dungeons, and other dimensions (the Nether and End).
  • CRAFTING & FARMING - Creating items, armor, and tools, as well as establishing farms for food and resources.
  • SURVIVAL - Managing hunger, fighting hostile mobs (monsters), and managing health.
  • ENGINEERING - Using redstone to create automated farms, traps, or logic computers.

    • CORE MULTIPLAYER FEATURES

  • COOPERATIVE & COMPETITIVE GAMEPLAY - Players can work together to build, farm, and explore, or engage in PvP (Player vs. Player) and various community-created minigames.
  • PERSISTENT WORLDS (REALMS) - Subscription-based, always-online servers that allow friends to join even when the host is offline.
  • SHARED INFRASTRUCTURE - Players can set up economies, towns, and shops to trade resources like diamonds and enchanted items.
  • IMPROVED COLLABORATION - Resources, farming, and building tasks can be divided, speeding up progression compared to single-player.

    • Quick Tip! -> Popular activities to start with include establishing a base with a bed and furnace, creating wheat and sugarcane farms for food and paper, and hunting for structures like villages or shipwrecks to find valuable treasure.
